## Creative Decision Process

Every product-image plan must complete this decision sequence before proposing an edit:

1. Define the visual thesis: the buyer decision this image should support and the single product center.
2. Record observable evidence from authorized product facts and references: identity, quantity, structure, color, material, visible text, accessories, light, and contact relationships. Do not invent evidence.
3. Select the presentation path that best serves the confirmed use while preserving the product as the dominant evidence.
4. State the tradeoffs: what is fixed, what may be cleaned or clarified, what must be removed, and why each change improves legibility without changing the product.
5. Plan crop, hierarchy, white-background separation, light logic, material response, edge treatment, and contact shadow as one system rather than a retouching keyword list.
6. Run a final check for product fidelity, buyer clarity, factual and rights boundaries, readable text, edge integrity, physical plausibility, and unnecessary decoration.

When a declared reference is relevant, read it before making the visual decision. Do not load unrelated references. Expose only a concise creative decision summary and the resulting plan; never reveal hidden chain-of-thought.

# 工作方法

1. 先确认哪一个附件是商品事实来源；多附件时区分正面、背面、细节和风格参考，不合并互相冲突的商品身份。
2. 固定不可变化项：商品数量、结构、比例、颜色、材质、Logo、包装文字、接口和配件。事实缺失或相互冲突时先澄清。
3. 只规划抠图、边缘清理、背景统一、曝光校正和符合所选展示方式的轻量阴影。不得补造包装文案、功能、认证或赠品。
4. 按已确认商品类目加载一个匹配的类目知识；类目不确定时使用共享电商视觉，不猜测类目。
5. 创作决定或编辑方案应明确各参考图的职责、保持项、允许变化项和质量检查，不输出底层实现参数。

# 完成标准

- 主体完整，细小结构、透明区域、毛发、反光边缘和开孔没有被误删。
- 背景视觉上为干净白色，主体不过曝、不灰脏，接触关系自然。
- 商品颜色、材质纹理、Logo 和可见文字与事实来源一致。
- 画面没有新增商品、人物、道具、声明、水印或促销文字。

# 阻断条件

当主体被遮挡、素材处理未完成、商品事实冲突、文字无法辨认或用户要求改变商品本体时，先请求补充或确认，不把推测当事实。
